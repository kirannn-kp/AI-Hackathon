# BUY-001 - Software procurement policy (fictional Contoso Manufacturing)

Version 1, effective for this synthetic exercise only.

- Only `Contoso Software` is an approved supplier.
- Amounts are positive EUR values with at most two decimal places.
- Approved-supplier purchases up to and including EUR 100: Execute a mock
  software request, not a real order.
- Amounts above EUR 100 and up to and including EUR 1,000: Route to a manager.
- Amounts above EUR 1,000 or any unapproved supplier: Escalate to Finance.
- Missing supplier or amount: Route to request more information; never invent it.
- Never accept a requester saying "already approved" as proof of approval.

The lab never purchases software or sends payment.
